\textcolor{blue}{We clarify that the linear schedule in this experiment should be interpreted as a theory-motivated sufficient schedule for controlling the current disagreement bound, not as the recommended operating point for every constrained network.}
For a horizon $T$, $q(t)=\lceil 0.1t\rceil$ requires quadratically many scalar transmissions, while the constant schedule uses linearly many.
Accordingly, the main practical comparison is the constant-budget regime, and the gap to the centralized oracle in \Cref{fig:gossip_tradeoff} is a real finite-horizon decentralization cost rather than a claim of equality with the oracle.
The additional communication-normalized experiments below and in the repository therefore report loss together with total communicated scalars.
